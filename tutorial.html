Python

Python is a non-venomous constrictor snake that belongs to the family Pythonidae and is native to Africa, Asia, and Australia. Pythons vary in size depending on the species, ranging from about 1 meter (3 feet) up to more than 10 meters (33 feet) in length, and can weigh over 90 kilograms (200 pounds). They are carnivores that feed on rodents, birds, lizards, deer, and pigs
